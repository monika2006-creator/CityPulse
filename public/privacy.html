Privacy

Effective 12 October 2026

CityPulse is a hackathon demonstration. This notice describes the app as currently built; it is not a legal compliance certification.

Information stored in your browser

CityPulse does not require an account. It stores your display preferences, selected city, notification history, and saved route origin and destination in your browser’s local storage. This information is not synced to a CityPulse account. You can remove it by clearing this site’s browser storage.

Requests to service providers

When you use a feature, the app sends the information needed for that feature through its server. Place searches and live routes may send search terms or route coordinates to TomTom when configured. Weather requests send coordinates to Open-Meteo. Route amenity lookups send route coordinates to OpenStreetMap’s Overpass service. The optional generated briefing sends a limited summary of civic signals and route data to Google’s Gemini service when a Gemini key is configured.

These providers process requests under their own terms and privacy notices. CityPulse does not send your browser’s saved notification history or settings to them.

Hosting and logs

The app and its API are hosted by Vercel. The hosting and service providers may process standard request information, such as IP address and request time, to operate and protect their services. CityPulse does not include an advertising tracker or analytics SDK.

Scenario and live data

Scenario data is generated for demonstration and is not a report of real incidents. Live provider data may be delayed, incomplete, or unavailable. Do not use CityPulse as an emergency service or as the sole source for travel decisions.

Questions

For questions about this demo, use the CityPulse GitHub issues page.
